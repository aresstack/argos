Annual Report 2025

Observing statistics

In 2025 the observatory recorded 212 clear nights, 31 more than in 2024. Of these, 148 nights were used for research programmes and 52 for public observing nights.

Visitors

The observatory welcomed 18,400 visitors, including 96 school groups.

Finances

The budget was balanced; the Halvard Reflector received a new mirror coating.
