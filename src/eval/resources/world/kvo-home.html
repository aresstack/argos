Welcome to the official website of the Kestrel Valley Observatory, a public research observatory in the hills above the Kestrel Valley. We run public observing nights, lectures for all ages and a small astronomy museum.

Visitor information

Opening hours: Saturday 18:00–23:30 (public observing night), Sunday 14:00–17:00 (museum only). The observatory is closed on weekdays except for booked school groups.

Admission: adults 9 euros, children under 14 free. Please check your ticket under "Ticket status" before you come.
